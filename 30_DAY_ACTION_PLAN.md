## 📝 CONTENT WRITER BRIEF

### Brand Voice Guidelines
- **Tone**: Professional yet warm, knowledgeable yet approachable
- **Style**: Informative but conversational, expertise without jargon
- **POV**: Second person ("you") for direct engagement
- **Length**: Be thorough but scannable (use subheadings, lists, short paragraphs)

### SEO Writing Guidelines
1. **Keyword Density**: 1-2% natural inclusion
2. **Keyword Placement**: H1, first paragraph, subheadings, conclusion
3. **Related Keywords**: Include semantic variations and synonyms
4. **Internal Links**: 5-10 contextual links per page
5. **Readability**: Flesch Reading Ease score 60-70 (8th-9th grade level)

### Required Elements Per Page
- [ ] Compelling H1 with target keyword
- [ ] Introduction with target keyword in first 100 words
- [ ] 5-7 H2 subheadings
- [ ] Bullet points or numbered lists
- [ ] FAQ section (5-10 questions)
- [ ] Strong call-to-action
- [ ] Social proof (testimonials, statistics)
- [ ] Meta description (150-160 characters)
- [ ] Alt text for all images
